## What I do

- **Contract engineering:** I embed with product teams as an augment-type developer, learn the stack fast, and ship alongside the team.
- **Legacy modernization:** Migrated 50k+ line codebases to TypeScript and tightened CI/CD pipelines so deploys stop being an event.
- **My own products:** Small, focused tools that do one job well. Most of them live under the [Klectr org](https://github.com/Klectr).

## What I'm building

### [Zimer](https://zimer.klectr.dev) — time tracking for invoicing
Timer to invoice in two clicks. Self-host free forever, or use the hosted version.
`Bun` · `HTMX` · `SQLite` · [Source](https://github.com/Klectr/zimer)

### [Zlern](https://zlern.com) — homeschool planning for families
Lessons, scheduling, and progress tracking, built for a real homeschool family and refined in daily use.
`React` · `TypeScript` · `Supabase` · [Source](https://github.com/Klectr/zlern)

### [Zlorb](https://zlorb.klectr.dev) — hands-free deploys
A lightweight CI/CD daemon in Rust. It watches your git repos, pulls when they change, and runs your build.
`Rust` · `systemd` · [Source](https://github.com/Klectr/zlorb)

### [Zlab](https://github.com/Klectr/zlab) — an infinite canvas for your ideas
Notes, text, and images on an infinite canvas. Local-first, installable as a PWA, with import and export when you need them.
`TypeScript` · `React` · `PWA` · [Source](https://github.com/Klectr/zlab)

### [Thai Resources](https://thai.klectr.dev) — learning to read Thai
The lessons and worksheets I'm using to learn to read Thai, published as I go.
[Source](https://github.com/Klectr/thai)
